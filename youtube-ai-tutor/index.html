Features

Everything You Need to Learn Smarter 🏆

Powerful tools designed for students, developers, and lifelong learners

🎥 Multi-Frame Smart Capture

Don't just capture a single frame - get the full picture. The extension captures a temporal sequence (T-X, T0, T+X) so the AI understands movement, code demonstrations, and evolving diagrams.

  • ✔ T-X / T0 / T+X - Context before, at, and after your question
  • ✔ Adjustable intervals - Set your preferred time window
  • ✔ Visual annotations - Draw arrows, circles, text on frames
  • ✔ Burn-in merging - Annotations fused into the image sent to AI

💻 Native Chrome Side Panel

Study without losing sight of your video. The native Chrome side panel keeps the AI tutor right beside your content - no more tab switching, no more lost context.

  • ✔ Side Panel API - Doesn't cover the video player
  • ✔ Live preview - See the exact transcription sent to AI
  • ✔ One-click recapture - Resync with current video position
  • ✔ Education levels - ELI5 to Expert mode

🤖 Context-Aware AI Responses

Get precise answers because the AI sees exactly what you see. It receives the video frame, surrounding context, transcription, and your visual annotations for incredibly accurate responses.

  • ✔ Visual context - AI "sees" the video frame
  • ✔ Transcription - Knows what was being said
  • ✔ Annotations - Understands your highlighted areas
  • ✔ LaTeX support - Perfect for math & formulas

📓 Multi-Notebook Organization

Organize your learning by subject, course, or project. Create multiple notebooks, search through them instantly, and never lose a valuable insight again.

  • ✔ Multiple notebooks - One per subject/course
  • ✔ Color-coded - Visual organization
  • ✔ Dual entries - AI conversations + free notes
  • ✔ Local IndexedDB - Persists across sessions

📤 Export in Any Format

Take your knowledge anywhere. Export complete notebooks as Markdown, JSON, HTML, or beautifully formatted PDF with syntax-highlighted code and timestamped screenshots.

  • ✔ Markdown - For Obsidian, Notion, GitHub
  • ✔ JSON - Raw data portability
  • ✔ HTML - Standalone styled web page
  • ✔ PDF - Premium study document layout

Privacy First

Your Data Stays Yours. Period. 🔒

No backend. No servers. No tracking. Everything happens in your browser, and your data never leaves your device.

🔒

100% Local Storage

All your API keys, conversations, notes, and screenshots are stored locally in your browser. We literally cannot access them even if we wanted to.

🌐

No Backend Servers

The extension talks directly from your browser to the AI provider's API. There are no intermediary servers, no data pipeline, nothing.
